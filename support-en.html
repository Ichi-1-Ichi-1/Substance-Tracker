About Substance Tracker

Private tracking for everything you put into your body
Substance Tracker is for people who track themselves closely: biohackers, athletes, self-optimizers and anyone taking supplements, vitamins, nootropics, peptides, hormones or medications. The app records what you take, when and how much – and how you feel. Half-lives, level curves and interaction alerts show what is active in your body and where the risks are.
Emergency: 112 (EU) · 911 (US/Canada) · 999 (UK) · 000 (Australia). In case of unconsciousness, breathing problems, seizure, chest pain or overheating, call for help immediately and say honestly what was taken.

Our principles

Data instead of gut feeling. See in black and white what works for you and what doesn't.

Safety built in. Interaction alerts and emergency numbers, without judgement. The app sells nothing, does not source substances, does not encourage use and gives no dosing recommendations.

Private by design. No account, no cloud, no tracking. All data stays on your device.

Honest about limits. Substance Tracker does not replace medical or pharmaceutical advice.

Where does the information come from?

Half-lives and interactions are approximate values from publicly available reference information: prescribing information (SmPC), safety warnings from medicines agencies (e.g. EMA, FDA), standard pharmacology literature and established harm-reduction combination charts. The rules are organised by drug class (e.g. opioids, benzodiazepines, MAO inhibitors, stimulants).

Values vary a lot between individuals (age, liver and kidney function, genetics, long-term use, dose). “No warning” does not mean “safe”. The estimated level curve is a simplified model. Translations were created carefully but not reviewed by medical professionals in every language; the German and English texts are authoritative.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Save a backup regularly via “More → Save backup”, e.g. to iCloud Drive.

I have a new phone – how do I transfer everything?

“Save backup” on the old device, “Load backup” on the new one.

A substance is missing from the reference.

You can log any substance freely. Let us know which one is missing.

A value or alert seems wrong.

Please contact us with a source – we will check and correct it.
