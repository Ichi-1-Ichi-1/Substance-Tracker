Privacy policy

Substance Tracker · Last updated: November 2026
In short: Substance Tracker does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. There is no account, no cloud, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays exclusively on your device.

2. What data does the app process?

The app stores the content you enter yourself – e.g. intakes, schedule, mood, journal, nutrition values and attached documents – in your device's local storage (IndexedDB). All processing happens exclusively on your device, including reading PDF documents.

This data is not transmitted to us or to third parties. We have no access to it and can neither view nor restore it.

3. Health data

Your entries may be health data within the meaning of Art. 9 GDPR. Since they never leave your device, we do not process this data. Protect your device with a passcode or Face ID / Touch ID.

4. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking

The app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, sets no tracking cookies and creates no profiles.

5. Delivery of the app files

[WEB VERSION ONLY:] The web version is delivered via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When loading the app files, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data such as the IP address (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See GitHub's privacy statement. After that the app runs offline; your entries are never transmitted.

[APP STORE VERSION ONLY:] The App Store version is distributed by Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies to downloads and updates.

6. Backups and export

Backups (JSON) and exports (CSV) are created only by you via your device's share function. You decide where to save or send them (e.g. iCloud Drive, your doctor); the terms of that service apply there.

7. Deletion

You can remove all data at any time under “More → Delete all data”. Deleting the app also removes all locally stored data.

8. Your rights

Since we do not store any personal data about you, we hold no data to which access, rectification or erasure requests could apply. If you have questions, contact us at the email above. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
